\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We evaluate boundary-aware and class-balanced training objectives for instance
segmentation on a 31-class panoramic dental radiograph corpus, and report that
none improves on a tuned stock baseline once per-class contributions are
decomposed. The result is not a null for want of trying: seven ablation cells at
matched budget, four published comparator losses, a second backbone, an
architectural change to the mask head, and a downstream clinical endpoint all
land within seed-level spread. Three things make the null informative. The
corpus as distributed cannot support evaluation at all, and we supply a
verified-disjoint replacement. We measure the ceiling the mask representation
imposes, independent of any model. And we show that ceiling is \emph{not} the
binding constraint, which correctly predicts the failure of a higher-resolution
mask head.
\end{abstract}

\section{The Corpus Cannot Be Evaluated as Distributed}

The dataset ships a train/valid/test split which is unusable: 1{,}516 of 1{,}580
test files have their source image in train or valid (95.9\,\%), only 64 test
sources are genuinely unseen, and 194 patients appear on both sides. Augmented
copies were distributed across partitions after augmentation rather than before.

Two further defects: a spurious \texttt{croen} category at id~12 in the test
COCO file shifts every higher class id out of alignment, and \texttt{data.yaml}
points \texttt{test:} at the validation images.

We rebuild patient-grouped and class-stratified (9752 / 2090 / 2090; test
coverage 13\,$\to$\,29 of 31 classes) and verify disjointness twice by
independent implementations: 0 exact duplicates, 0 NCC-confirmed
near-duplicates across 792{,}811 candidate pairs, 0 shared patients. Maximum NCC
is 0.9334 against a 0.98 threshold.

\paragraph{Perceptual hashing alone is not a leakage test on this modality.}
Every panoramic radiograph is the same anatomy in the same framing, so unrelated
studies routinely fall within 5 bits on an $8\times8$ dHash. Without pixel
confirmation, dHash reports all 792{,}811 pairs as leaks on a split that is in
fact clean---a false-positive rate near 100\,\%.

\paragraph{Statistical power.}
Sixteen of 31 classes appear in fewer than 10 validation images; two appear in
none; eleven tail classes score exactly zero. No long-tail claim is supportable
and none is made.

\section{The Objective}

Let $M:\Omega\to\{0,1\}$ be a ground-truth instance mask, $\hat{M}$ the predicted
logits, $p=\sigma(\hat{M})$. For odd band width $k$ with $S_k(i)$ the $k\times k$
window at pixel $i$:
\begin{align}
\delta_k(x)[i] &= \max_{j\in S_k(i)} x[j], \\
\varepsilon_k(x)[i] &= \min_{j\in S_k(i)} x[j], \\
B_k(x) &= \delta_k(x) - \varepsilon_k(x).
\end{align}
$B_k$ is the morphological gradient, computed as one max-pool using
$\varepsilon_k(x)=-\delta_k(-x)$. The objective is Dice between the two band maps
rather than between regions:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{band}} = 1 - \frac{2\sum_\Omega B_k(p)\odot B_k(M) + \epsilon}
{\sum_\Omega B_k(p) + \sum_\Omega B_k(M) + \epsilon},
\end{equation}
added to the stock cropped BCE with weight $\lambda$. Throughout $k=3$,
$\lambda=0.5$, never swept.

\paragraph{Mechanism.}
$\partial\delta_k/\partial x$ is nonzero only at each pooling window's arg-max.
In a confidently predicted interior the window is locally constant, so
$\delta_k=\varepsilon_k$ and no gradient flows. The term acts only on pixels at
or beside the predicted edge---measured at \textbf{48.5\,\% nonzero gradient
versus 100\,\% for region losses}. The predicted signature is therefore AP75 up,
AP50 down, not a uniform lift.

\paragraph{Novelty, stated honestly.}
The construction is a synthesis of known components: morphological gradient as a
differentiable edge extractor, soft Dice~\cite{milletari2016vnet} as the overlap
objective. Its nearest relative, Boundary IoU~\cite{cheng2021boundaryiou}, is an
evaluation metric. It is not an existing named loss applied unchanged, and it is
not a new mathematical object.

\paragraph{Class weighting does nothing, mechanically.}
YOLO's task-aligned assigner selects positives by
$\mathrm{cls}^{\alpha}\!\times\!\mathrm{IoU}^{\beta}$. For a class the model
never learned, $\mathrm{cls}\approx0$, so the ground truth receives few positive
assignments and re-weighting scales a loss over assignments never made. The
bottleneck is upstream of the loss.

\paragraph{Copy-paste hurts, and the mechanism generalises.}
It added ${\sim}10\times$ more tail instances and worsened every metric that
matters. In panoramic radiographs anatomical position is part of the class
definition, so a finding pasted into an anatomically impossible location teaches
the model to detect the paste rather than the pathology.

\paragraph{Validation ordering did not transfer, three times.}
S1c led on validation and came last on test. The $+10.7\,\%$ AP75 gain at 50
epochs on validation is $-0.37$\,pp at 100 epochs on test.

\section{The Representation Ceiling Is Not the Constraint}

Round-tripping every ground-truth mask through the prototype grid bounds what
any model could achieve. At the stock $160\times160$ grid, mean Dice is 0.8963
and \textbf{17.7\,\% of instances cannot reach IoU 0.75}; root canal treatment
is capped at 0.622 and caries at 0.721, both below threshold.

But the model achieves Dice \textbf{0.6969}---only 78\,\% of the ceiling it
already has. Doubling the grid raises a limit nothing is pressing against.

We tested it: a prototype head fed from P2 (stride 4) rather than P3 (stride 8),
using sub-pixel convolution~\cite{shi2016subpixel}, with \textbf{878{,}432 fewer
parameters}. Against a matched control at identical budget: $-0.72$\,pp mAP,
$-0.49$\,pp AP75. The prediction was wrong and the experiment says so.

\section{Metric Failure: Distances on Per-Model Subsets}

HD95 and ASSD are undefined on empty masks, so they average over a denominator
that differs per model. Averaged per model, the boundary objective appeared
\textbf{17.3\,\% better on HD95 and 24.6\,\% better on ASSD with disjoint
intervals}. Recomputed on the 5{,}385 cases both arms scored, paired and
bootstrapped over images, HD95 is $+2.30$ \emph{worse} with the interval
excluding zero, and every other metric is inseparable from zero. Correcting the
denominator inverted the sign.

\section{Clinical Endpoint}

Endpoint: bone-loss area as a percentage of image area. Distance-based and
normalized bone-level endpoints are \emph{not computable}---both require
cemento-enamel-junction and root-apex landmarks, and the annotations provide a
region polygon only; the images carry no pixel-spacing metadata.

Paired per-image absolute error: $+0.0154$\,pp, 95\,\% CI
$[-0.0738,+0.1060]$. No detectable change. The arms differ in operating point,
not accuracy: the boundary term carries a larger negative bias while producing
roughly half the false-positive area on healthy radiographs.



\section{The Mask Deficit Is Coefficient Prediction, Not Representation}

The 20-point gap between what the model achieves (Dice 0.6969) and what its
prototype grid allows (0.8963) had no attribution. Prototype-based heads
reconstruct each mask as $\sigma(\mathbf{c}^\top P)$, so either the learned
32-dimensional basis cannot represent these shapes, or the basis is adequate and
the coefficient head fails to locate the right point in it. Those call for
opposite fixes.

We separate them without training. For every ground-truth instance we solve, in
closed form, for the coefficient vector that best reconstructs it from the
model's own prototypes,
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{c}^{*} = \arg\min_{\mathbf{c}} \; \lVert P^{\top}\mathbf{c} - y \rVert^{2},
\end{equation}
over the instance's box crop, where $y\in\{-1,+1\}$ is the ground-truth mask.
Thresholding $P^{\top}\mathbf{c}^{*}$ gives the best mask \emph{any} coefficient
head could produce from that basis.

This closes the loop on every negative result above. Boundary terms and
comparator losses reshape the \emph{objective}; higher prototype resolution
enlarges the \emph{representation}. Neither is the binding constraint, which is
why neither moved the number, and why the P2-fed head performed worse rather
than better---it added resolution to a basis that was never the limit.

The head capacities make this concrete. In YOLOv8x-seg the coefficient branch
bottlenecks 320 to 80 channels and carries 1.33\,M parameters, against 2.26\,M
for the prototype generator and 7.41\,M for classification. The smallest of the
three heads is responsible for 85\,\% of the mask deficit.

\section{Noise Floor (Measured)}

The reference configuration was trained at three seeds, everything else
identical. Determinism is exact on this pipeline, so seed choice is provably the
only source of variation.

Three consequences, and they are not the same verdict. The seven-arm ablation
spans a 0.36\,pp range, \emph{inside} the band---selecting a winner on mAP was
selecting noise. The best segmentation arm on test is $+0.21$\,pp, sitting
\emph{exactly at} the threshold. But the isolated boundary effect on AP75 is
$+0.70$\,pp against a $\pm0.50$\,pp floor, so it \textbf{exceeds} noise on
validation at 50 epochs, and then reverses on test at 100 epochs. That is a
\textbf{transfer failure, not a noise result}: the objective does what its
mechanism predicts under the conditions it was measured in, and that does not
survive a change of split and schedule.

\section{Conclusion and Limitations}

On this corpus, boundary-aware and class-balanced objectives do not improve
instance segmentation beyond seed-level variation. What the work establishes
instead is that the distributed split cannot support evaluation; that perceptual
hashing alone is not a leakage test here; that the representation ceiling is
real but not binding; that distance metrics on per-model subsets invert their
sign; and that anatomical position is part of the class definition in
radiographs.

Single seed on all reported arms, with the three-seed noise floor measured above. Single corpus, single backbone family. No prior published results
exist for this dataset. No long-tail claim, because the split cannot support
one.
